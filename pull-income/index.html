Pull Income

How many pulls you earn per day, week and month, with and without an Our Notes pass.

Saving up

Pulls that count

Event tickets are 3 Our Notes tickets per event, with four events a month.

Star pulls are Stars divided by 200. Average daily income may be inaccurate until more data is gathered.

Each pass option is one pass on its own. It is not stacked with the others.

The Premium Mission Pass can be added to any option, F2P included. The free Season Mission Pass track is always counted. The Season Mission Pass counts in the monthly column only.

How income is calculated

Base income (everyone)

  • Daily: login 20 + daily mission 15 = 35 Stars
  • Weekly: 35 × 7 + weekly stamp 160 + one event (360 reward + 400 story) = 1,165 Stars
  • Monthly: 35 × 30 + stamp 160 × 4 + events 760 × 4 + Season Mission Pass free track 200 = 4,930 Stars

Turning income into pulls

  • Star pulls = Stars ÷ 200
  • Event tickets = 3 per event: 3 a week, 12 a month, none counted daily
  • Exclusive and Dedicated pulls are weekly, so they count ×4 in a month
  • Pickup pulls (Premium) = 1 × 7 a week, 1 × 30 a month
  • Total = star pulls + event tickets + Exclusive + Dedicated + Pickup
  • Our Notes only = star pulls + event tickets
  • Pickup only = star pulls + Pickup pulls
